FAQ

How does Ascent measure my jump?

It times how long you're in the air, the same way jump mats do, and works out the height from that. Pocket uses your phone's motion sensors. Vision and Upload find your takeoff and landing in the video.

Do I need any equipment?

No, just an iPhone. For Vision, something to lean it on (a tripod, a bag, a bench) helps.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Videos are analysed on your phone and stay there.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhones running iOS 17.6 or later. Android isn't supported yet.
